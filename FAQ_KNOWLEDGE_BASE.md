## 💰 1. Salary, Payouts & Rates

### Cut-off Schedules & Payout Dates
- **Cut-off Cycle**: Cut-offs follow standard bi-monthly / monthly project cycles.
- **Minimum Hours**: Workers must complete at least 20 to 40 hours within the cut-off period to qualify for live task payouts.
- **Distribution Timeline**: Payouts are reviewed and distributed once the project checking and audit period closes.

### Salary Delays & Status
- **Why is payout pending?**: Payout processing involves batch verification between Judgers and Admin to prevent fraud. Wait for the announcement in the official community group before filing an inquiry.

### Withdrawal Process & Minimums
- **Bank / E-Wallet**: Payouts are made to the registered GCash or Bank account in your dashboard.
- **Accuracy**: Payout details must match your legal name. No withdrawals to dummy or placeholder accounts are permitted.

### Task Rates & Earnings
- **Base Rate**: $2 per 800 qualified features. Experienced labelers earn $3 to $6+ per hour depending on speed and accuracy.
- **Building vs. Landcover**: Rates vary by active project topology and complexity.

## 🛠️ 2. Ginger App & Technical Setup

### Mobile / Cellphone Compatibility
- **Question**: Puwede po ba ang Ginger sa cellphone or Android?
- **Answer**: **HINDI po puwede sa cellphone.** Ang Ginger software ay eksklusibo para lamang sa Windows PC o Laptop.

### Auto-Update & Version Errors
- **Error**: *"Invalid software version"* or *"Download failed"* during update.
- **Resolution**:
  1. Close Ginger completely.
  2. Turn on a VPN (e.g. ProtonVPN set to Singapore / SG server).
  3. Re-open Ginger and let the auto-update finish downloading.

### App Freeze / Crash & USER Folder Cleanup
- **Issue**: Ginger freezes when zooming in, crashes in 3D mode, or says *"Not Responding"*.
- **Resolution**:
  1. Open File Explorer and navigate to your Ginger installation directory.
  2. Delete or clear the `USER` and `LOG` folders.
  3. Restart Ginger. Note: This clears the local view cache without losing your account history.

### VC-Redist Installation
- **Setup**: Install VC-Redist from the onboarding module. When prompted for mirrors/servers, choose Singapore (SG) or the server with the highest percentage.

## 🎓 3. Training & Onboarding

### Pausing Training & Resuming Next Day
- **Rule**: **Puwede po ipagpatuloy bukas.** Training is self-paced (*at your own pace*). Your completed modules are saved and will not be wiped if you pause for the night.

### Training Timer Timeout
- **Rule**: If the countdown timer expires while you are in a training task, the timer simply resets for that specific task. You do not get penalized or kicked out.

### Submission Shortcuts
- **Ctrl + A**: Submit completed task.
- **Ctrl + D**: Acquire / fetch next task.
- **Ctrl + T**: Detect topology errors in the road/building layout.

### Next Training Module Unlocking
- After completing Standard Building, proceed to the Active Projects tab to register and unlock Landcover / Roof Training.

## 🔑 4. Account & Credentials

### Ginger Login (Username & Password)
- **Where to find**: Go to your Dashboard website > Click **Account Info**.
- **Requirement**: Your Ginger credentials will only appear after you finish the required onboarding modules.

### Labeler ID
- **Location**: Your unique Labeler ID is displayed at the top of your **Account Info** page.

### Locked Account Info
- If your Account Info shows a lock icon, you still have incomplete pre-course modules or verification steps. Finish all videos and quizzes first.

### OTP & Verification
- If SMS OTP is delayed, the cellular gateway may be undergoing maintenance. Try email verification or check again shortly.

## 📋 5. Projects & Tasks

### Checker & Judger Assignment
- Once training tasks are submitted, they are queued for team checkers. Checking speed depends on the active queue volume.
- Checkers will review your work and provide feedback or mark your account as verified.

### Live Task Pulling
- You may only pull live tasks on projects where you have passed the prerequisite exam and training. Do not pull tasks for unverified modules.
